This site collects what we write about our work in the [Evref-BL](https://github.com/Evref-BL) organization:

- **Guides** walk through doing something with our tools, and are kept up to date.
- **Experiments** say what we tried, how we measured it and what we found.
- **Projects** present one of our projects: what it does, why it exists and where it is going.
- **News** announces releases, talks and papers.

Every list on the site shows one line per post, so you can scan it and open what interests you. Posts can also be browsed [by tag](/tags/), or [searched](/search/).

## For agents

Every post is also served as Markdown: add `.md` to its path. The [index for agents](/llms.txt) lists every post with a link to its Markdown, and [one file](/llms-full.txt) holds them all.

## Writing for this site

Anyone in the team can write a post: see [how to write a post](/posts/writing-a-post/).
